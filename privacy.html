Privacy Policy & Wellness Disclaimer

Application: SteadyHands — Fidget, Focus & Urge Relief (com.phantasticvanguard.fidget)
Developer / Publisher: Phantastic.Vanguard LLC
Effective Date: October 23, 2026

Summary: SteadyHands runs 100% on your device and works completely offline. We collect zero personal information, use zero third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and never transmit your usage data off your device.

1. Information We Collect (Zero Data Collection)

SteadyHands does not require an account, login, or internet connection after installation. Specifically:

  • Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or user IDs.
  • Location Data: We never request or access GPS or network location.
  • Device & Advertising IDs: We do not read Advertising IDs, IMEI, MAC addresses, or fingerprint your device.
  • Analytics & Crash Reporting: No third-party tracking scripts, telemetry, or ad networks are bundled in the app.

2. On-Device Local Storage & Data Deletion

To remember your preferred sensory palette, sound/haptics settings, Low-Stimulation mode, and local fidget/urge counters between sessions, the app uses standard on-device browser storage (localStorage).

  • This data remains strictly on your device and is never uploaded to any server.
  • You can erase all saved local counters and preferences at any time by tapping the 🛡️ Info button in the top bar and pressing "Reset Local Data & Counters", or by clearing app storage/uninstalling the application.

3. Device Permissions

  • Vibration / Haptics (VIBRATE): Used solely to generate gentle tactile pulses when you interact with fidget controls or breathing pacers. You can toggle haptics off at any time in the top bar.
  • Web Audio Synthesis: All clicks, pops, chimes, worry-stone hums, and ambient brown/rain noise are synthesized mathematically in real time on your device. No microphone access is ever requested or used.

4. Health, Sensory & Wellness Non-Medical Disclaimer

SteadyHands is designed as a general self-regulation, busy-hands focus, and 3-minute craving distraction companion for everyday use (including sensory-friendly low-stimulation fidgeting and paced breathing exercises).

  • Not a Medical Device: SteadyHands is not a medical device, clinical diagnostic tool, psychotherapy program, or smoking cessation pharmaceutical product.
  • No Medical Advice: Nothing in this application constitutes medical, clinical, or psychological advice, nor is it intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease, disorder, or addiction.
  • Always seek the advice of a physician or qualified healthcare provider regarding any medical condition, neurodevelopmental support plan, or smoking cessation program.

5. Children's Privacy

Because SteadyHands collects zero personal data, zero device identifiers, and displays zero advertisements, no personal information from any user of any age is ever collected, stored, or shared by Phantastic.Vanguard LLC.
